Background/rationale: 
 Lepcha language is spoken in Sikkim, Darjeeling, Kalimpong district of 
west Bengal, in Ilam district in Nepal, and a few villages of Samtsi district in South-
west Bhutan. Today the Lepcha people constitute a minority of the population of 
modern Sikkim. In Sikkim, Lepcha is one of the eleven official languages, Lepcha is 
taught at the primary school level up to University, and the Ph.D. course has already 
started. There is a textbook section in the education department Government of 
Sikkim that has developed official learning materials, there is a Lepcha edition of a 
government newspaper, Sikkim Herald and the government radio station broadcast 
news bulletins and cultural programs in the Lepcha languages. 
Learning objectives: 
To develop four language skills 
To decode, analyze, interpret the concept, and explore it 
To develop reasoning imagination and critical thinking, etc. 
To Apply language appropriately using the correct structure of grammar and 
accurate and fluency

style with a greater appreciation
