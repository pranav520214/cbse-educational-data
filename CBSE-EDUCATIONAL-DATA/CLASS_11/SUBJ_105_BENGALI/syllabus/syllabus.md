Background- 
Bengali and its cultural background is an important chapter of Indian history. Bengali language has a rich tradition 
of its own. From Charyapada to modern Bengali language the journey is quite interesting. So one has to know the 
language to understand the cultural heritage of Bengal. Bengali language developed since the 10th century and 
the contribution of many great poets, writers, dramatists, linguists are to be known sincerely. This very language 
and its study will play a critical role in inculcating National and emotional integration in the minds of young 
students of secondary senior secondary level. The language curriculum should be created in such a way that it 
helps to develop creativity and imaginative power among the students. Similarly, it should help them with a 
powerful communication skill in both speaking and reading, writing activity. 
 
Objectives 
The objectives of teaching Bengali at senior secondary stage are: 
1. To know the language which is very rich in its own tradition. 
2. To build a sense of confidence in the students in both oral and written form of Bengali language. 
3. To know the dialects and sociolect of Bengali language and use it in appropriate social and regional 
settings. 
4. To equip the learners with adequate language skill to communicate, to question and in necessary to raise 
their voice against anything immoral. 
5. According to CBSE & NEP, students will be able to prepare themselves to meet 21st century’s challenges and develop their 
capabilities for CBE. 
6. To help them in developing both performance and competence areas of the language and imparting 
information through consulting dictionaries, using internet, getting a book in library on their own. 
7. To help them acquiring different registers of Bengali language integrating various form of Arts to enrich 
themselves. 
8. To develop a sense of curiosity for extensive reading and to facilitate self-learning, so that the students 
become independent in their learning process. 
9. They will also be able to edit and review their own work. 
10. So the students will be able to not only communicate in Bengali, they can have other things to do, as 
well. Such as can understand the advertisements, news, weather forecasts, interviews etc 
11. They can also narrate pictorial story easily, write formal letters and can individually do official 
correspondence. 
12. This syllabus will make the students competent in Bengali language in an interesting way.
