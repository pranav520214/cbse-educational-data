1. Background/Rationale 
As a syllabus provides a framework for the assessment of teacher and learner achievement, it 
is an inseparable part of the course of the study. It also specifies the work in course setting and it 
is a summary of the curriculum also. 
With a perspective to enable the students to acquire a reasonable degree of language 
proficiency in Manipuri, the framework for the assessment of teacher and learner achievement 
has been prepared. The course will aim, mainly at promoting the higher order language skills. By 
the time the students completed the higher secondary level, i.e. Class XI, they are expected to 
achieve the skills for text-based writing, able to write formal/informal letters and applications for 
different purposes, grammatical rules of Manipuri language, etc. 
After the completion of the course, a large number of students are going to pursue for the 
graduation courses in the respective subject, and they should be well-prepared for this. A 
reasonable degree of proficiency in the relevant subject is very much needed for the higher-up 
studies. Thus, the course will mainly give focus on the required language skills for the academic 
study. 
2. Learning objectives 
The main learning objectives in this stage are: 
 To achieve greater knowledge and proficiency in the use of language skills necessary 
both for the social and academic purposes. 
 To develop basic skill and new ideas by introducing the text-based answers of the unseen 
passages. 
 To improve their communication, social and handwriting skills and to understand the 
meaning of Phrase and Idioms. 
 To identify different word and understand compose examples of grammar elements. 
 To identify literary techniques and creative uses of language in literary text. To identify the 
central/main points and supporting details, etc. 
 To achieve the advanced language skills with an aim to develop the skills of reasoning, 
drawing inferences through meaningful activities. 
 To develop the capacity to appreciate literary use of Manipuri and also use Manipuri 
creatively and imaginatively.

6. Guidelines for internal assessment (Practical/ Projects etc.) 
A total of 20 marks have been allotted for the Internal Assessment. Out of the 20 marks 
10 is for Speaking and Listening competency and 10 is allotted for Assignments/Note-Book 
/Project Works. Assignments/Note-Books/Project Works are given to the concerned students as 
per the syllabus of the subject. On the basis of the competency, knowledge on the relevant topics, 
punctuality of the submission, etc. of the student, with due care and monitor, the marks are 
awarded.
