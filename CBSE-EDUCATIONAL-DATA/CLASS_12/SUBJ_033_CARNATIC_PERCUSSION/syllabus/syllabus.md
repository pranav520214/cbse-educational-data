2. Choice Tala: The student should be asked to play full-fledged tani

3. Tala of Examiner’s choice: The student should be asked to play tani 
avartanam to one or two talas of the examiner’s choice not necessarily the 
whole course of the tani avartanam but only selected phrases. 
4. The candidate may be asked to recite Mohra, Korvai, Tirmanam for 
different talas learnt. 
5. The candidate should be asked to accompany vocal music, instrumental music 
or both. The music may be provided either by the examiners themselves or by 
an artist specifically engaged for the purpose.
